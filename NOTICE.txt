IPA Filler (Anki add-on) - notices and attribution
====================================================

Pronunciation data
------------------
The IPA transcriptions this add-on looks up come from Wiktionary, written by
the Wiktionary contributors (https://en.wiktionary.org). They are licensed
under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International licence
(CC BY-SA 4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

The add-on retrieves them through:
  - FreeDictionaryAPI.com (https://freedictionaryapi.com), which serves
    parsed English Wiktionary entries, and
  - the Wiktionary (MediaWiki) API (https://en.wiktionary.org/w/api.php).

Changes: the IPA written into your notes is adapted from that material. It
is normalised to a British learner's-dictionary style (for example `ɹ` to
`r`, British vowel symbols, syllable dots, optional sounds, diacritics and
stress on one-syllable words removed, weak forms of function words in
phrases), and phrases may be assembled word by word. The adapted IPA remains
under CC BY-SA 4.0. If you share or publish notes filled this way, credit
"Wiktionary contributors", link to the licence, and keep that IPA under
CC BY-SA 4.0.

Claude output (optional)
------------------------
When you enable the Claude fallback, IPA for headwords the free sources
missed is generated by Anthropic's Claude API, using your own Anthropic API
key and account, under Anthropic's terms. Such notes are tagged ipa::llm.
The add-on contacts api.anthropic.com only when you enable this.
